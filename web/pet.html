Clawd, desktop pet

Clawd lives on top of every window on your screen. When you don't need him he wanders, hops onto window edges, fidgets and watches your cursor. Pick him up and drop him anywhere (shake him and he gets dizzy), double-click for a hop, click a window and he'll notice. Hover him to peek at his box, click to pin his recent activity, Esc to close.
